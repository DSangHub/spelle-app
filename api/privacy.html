Privacy policy

Last updated November 14, 2026.

Spelle is a paste desk for translating and checking a social-media comment. This page explains what the site collects and what it does not.

What you paste

The comment and the reply you type are sent to the Spelle server so they can be translated and checked. They are not stored in a database, not linked to an account, and not used to train a model. Once the reply is returned, Spelle does not keep the text.

Accounts and payments

A free test run does not require an account. If you later create a profile or pay for a plan, Spelle stores your email, the language you chose, and the payment status. Payments are handled by Stripe. Spelle does not store your card number.

Cookies and ads

Spelle itself uses the browser’s local storage to remember your language choices on this device. If an advertising partner is added, that partner may set its own cookies to measure views and show relevant ads. Those cookies are controlled by the ad company, not by Spelle. You can refuse them in your browser settings.

Analytics

Spelle does not run its own tracking pixels. Server logs may include the usual technical details of a request (time, path, and a truncated address) for a short period so the site can stay up.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the address on the Spelle site.
