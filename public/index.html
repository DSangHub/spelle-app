Built for the reply you would have skipped.

You can follow the thread. You should not have to post in it.

Spelle is the bridge for people who are not fluent in the language of the comment. No new social account. No API key. No browser extension fighting the app. You stay in the app you opened, and Spelle only holds the text long enough to translate and check it.

What Spelle does not do

It does not post for you. Social apps do not allow a third app to drop a reply into a thread. Copy and paste is the reliable path, and it is the whole product. It also does not ask you to bring an AI key. The key stays with Spelle.

Spell-checked means ready to send.

A raw machine translation still looks like a raw machine translation. The last step is a spelling and polish pass: names stay names, the reply stays as long as you wrote it, and slang is only softened if you ask. Then one tap copies it.
